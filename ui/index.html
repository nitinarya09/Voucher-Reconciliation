💡 Plain-English Guide: How Treasury (IFMIS) & AG (VLC) Records Balance Out
▲ Collapse Guide
❓ Why do small differences (e.g. ₹250 or ₹800) occur?
When an employee repays an advance (Festival/Grain) or pays Professional Tax (₹250), Treasury books full gross salary and lists recovery under deductions. AG compiler nets this recovery directly on the bill. Both result in the exact same bank payment.
❓ Why is ₹1,250 Crore (MH 4801) not in the Treasury File?
High-value power capital subsidies are processed at the State Secretariat via a direct Letter of Credit (LC) / Book Transfer approved by the Finance Department, so they bypass ordinary district treasury counters.
❓ What are Public Account Heads (MH 8011, 8342, 8658)?
These represent employee insurance (GIS), retirement savings (NPS), and income tax deposits (TDS). The government does not spend this money—it holds it in trust like a bank. Therefore, AG compiles them in banking ledgers rather than payment accounts.
❓ What does 100% Mathematical Parity mean?
It proves that after accounting for the statutory bookkeeping differences above, every single rupee between the Treasury and the Accountant General matches to ₹0.00 exact discrepancy.

💸 Refund Vouchers & Deduct-Expenditure Register 50 Entries
Statutory Accounting of Refund Vouchers in Indian Government Accounts:
• 1. Deduct-Expenditure on Vouchers (Advance Recoveries): In COMPAY, advance recoveries (Festival Advance SDCD=012, Grain Advance SDCD=017 under MH 7610) are entered as negative monetary amounts directly against salary vouchers. In IFMIS, these recoveries are scheduled under File 3 (AG Deductions). This causes voucher gross to be lower in COMPAY by the exact recovery amount.
• 2. Refund of Revenue / Tax Challans (Minor Head 900): Tax refunds (Commercial Tax, Stamp Duty, Transport) are booked under Minor Head 900 (Deduct Refunds) under Revenue Major Heads (e.g. 2040-00-900). In government compilation, these entries deduct from revenue receipts rather than inflating budget expenditures.
• 3. Non-Cash Transfer Entry (TE) Refunds: Inter-head debit/credit adjustments passed by the AG via Transfer Entries (e.g. TE 54, TE 61 under MH 8658 / 8671) to adjust misclassifications or refund credit balances.

📖 Indian Government Accounts & Treasury Acronym Glossary

Statutory definitions, Major Heads, and accounting explanations for abbreviations used across the six files and reconciliation registers.

Acronym Full Accounting Title Associated Major Head Statutory Purpose & Treatment in Reconciliation
PT Professional Tax (Profession Tax) MH 0028 (Taxes on Income & Exp) Tax levied by the State Government on salaried employment and professions. Deducted monthly from salaries at source in IFMIS (File 4). In AG compilation, occasionally netted directly from gross expenditure.
TDS Tax Deducted at Source (Income Tax) MH 8658.112 (TDS Suspense) Central Income Tax withheld from employee salaries and vendor payments at the time of bill passing. Retained at the treasury level under Suspense Head 8658 for electronic remittance.
GPF General Provident Fund MH 8009 (State Provident Funds) Mandatory statutory retirement savings of government employees. Deducted from pay bills (File 3) and compiled by the Accountant General as credit deposits under Public Account Major Head 8009 in COMPAY (File 6).
GIS Group Insurance Scheme MH 0216 / 8011 (Insurance & Pension) State government employees group life insurance and savings deduction retained at treasury level.
VLC Voucher Level Classification AG Compilation System Computerized accounting database system maintained at the Office of the Accountant General (A&E) to post, classify, compile, and balance monthly state accounts from treasury vouchers.
IFMIS Integrated Financial Management Information System State Treasury Portal State Government electronic web portal through which DDOs submit bills and treasuries pass, verify, and electronically disburse payments.
SOP Schedule of Payments Treasury Monthly Return Official chronological schedule of all vouchers passed and disbursed by the treasury during the accounting month, submitted to the Accountant General along with supporting vouchers.
COMPAY Compiled Payments Dataset VLC Reference Table Database dump extracted from VLC containing all posted expenditure vouchers and non-cash Transfer Entries compiled by the AG for a specific treasury month.
DDO Drawing and Disbursing Officer Departmental Entity Designated gazetted government officer empowered to draw public money from the treasury for departmental expenditure. Identified by a unique 10-digit code.
MH / MHCD Major Head / Major Head Code 4-digit functional code Top level in the 15-digit Indian Government budget classification structure (e.g., 2013: Council of Ministers, 2052: Secretariat, 8009: GPF).
TE Transfer Entry (Journal Adjustment) Non-cash Book Adjustment Correction or inter-departmental book adjustment passed in the AG office (marked with SS_SOP = 'TE' in COMPAY). Adjusts figures without generating bank payment cheques.
FVC Fully Vouched Contingent Bill Detailed Contingency Payment bill for office supplies, labour, or services supported by detailed sub-vouchers and receipts.
